Boss Maker

Turn your own character into the final boss in Super Mario Construct. Add your poses, test every move on the stage, then download your boss.

STEP 1

Add your poses

Draw your character in the template (Piskel or Pixilart work great), or upload separate pictures. Draw your character 56 pixels tall or less, with the feet touching the bottom of the box. At that size it shows up in the game pixel for pixel, as sharp as the real enemies. Only Idle 1 is required. Empty slots borrow another pose.

    STEP 2

    Test your moves

    Boss style:

    The dashed box shows the player's size so you can see how big your boss is. This is the real in-game size, zoomed in. Drawings taller than 58 pixels get shrunk to exactly 1/2 (or 1/3), which keeps hard edges but loses detail.

    STEP 3

    Download your boss

    1. Name the zip with your code name (like dragonqueen), not your real name.
    2. Turn the zip in to your teacher.
    3. Your teacher adds your boss to the class Boss Picker site, or sends back a mod folder for the LSS Mod Loader.
    4. On the Boss Picker site, click your boss and hit Fight. With the Mod Loader: click Manager, upload your mod folder, and click Load Modpack.
    5. Put Bowser in a level. That's your boss now.
